# Sales skills (third-party)

Twelve skills from [louisblythe/sales-skills](https://github.com/louisblythe/sales-skills) (commit e0f13a6, MIT licence), copied unchanged:

copywriting, copy-editing, sales-psychology, storytelling, objection-handling, social-proof-injection, urgency-creation, scarcity-urgency-calibration, ab-message-testing, analytics-tracking, customer-referrals, email-sequence.

They were written for sales teams and bots. On Landscape, use them for site copy, the digest, launch posts and partner outreach, under these rules:

- Real urgency only: tender deadlines, the survey close date, this week's updates. No fake countdowns or "only N spots left".
- Proof comes from the data (company, update and tender counts built into `site/data/`). Never invent testimonials, quotes or numbers.
- One main call to action per page: the weekly digest.
- Hebrew copy follows CLAUDE.md: gender-neutral, the directory is "חברות", market updates are "עדכונים".
- Email follows the anti-spam law (§30A): consent first, sender identity in every footer, one-click unsubscribe. No cold sequences to scraped addresses.
