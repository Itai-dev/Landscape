# Research sources

Good places to discover and verify organizations. Add to this list as runs find better ones.

## Directories & databases
- ~~Startup Nation Central Finder~~ — shut down Aug 2026; use startupim.com, IVC Research Center, Dealroom instead
- Crunchbase — funding, founders, HQ
- LinkedIn company pages — size bands, locations
- Clutch / DesignRush / Sortlist — agency & studio listings (filter Israel)
- IMDb company credits — production, VFX, post on Israeli films & series

## Portfolios & awards
- Behance, Vimeo, Dribbble, ArtStation (search "Israel", "Tel Aviv")
- Awwwards / CSSDA (Israel), Motion Awards, Annecy / Animix Tel Aviv
- Effie Israel, Cannes Lions Israeli entries, Golden Pencil (Kipod)
- Ophir Awards (Israeli Academy) credits

## Press
- Geektime, CTech (Calcalist English), Globes, TheMarker, Ynet, Haaretz
- ICE (ice.co.il) and Globes marketing — ad agency account moves, campaign credits
- Portfolio magazine (prtfl.co.il) — Israeli design & studios

## Signal pages (checked every cycle)
- Globes ad agencies tag — https://www.globes.co.il/news/משרדי_פרסום.tag (account moves, best source)
- ICE advertising & marketing — https://www.ice.co.il/advertising-marketing (ad of the week = full credits)
- Geektime funding — https://www.geektime.co.il/category/funding/
- Globes English front page — https://en.globes.co.il
- CTech (Calcalist English) funding rounds
- LinkedIn public job search: "motion designer", "3D", "animator", "creative director" in Israel (relative dates → `date_approx`)
- Globes "הרשת החברתית" tag — https://www.globes.co.il/news/הרשת_החברתית.tag (Galit Hatan's ad-industry items: account moves, campaigns)
- ICE appointments — https://www.ice.co.il/positions ; marketing appointments — https://shivuk.me/news/marketingnews (relative dates; open each item for the exact date)
- Portfolio "מה הולך" weekly — https://www.prtfl.co.il (design/illustration/animation events and festivals)
- mr.gov.il exemption notices (`s=EXEMPTION`, words קמפיין / הפקת): LPM extensions name the production company per campaign → signals
- Not reliable yet: gov.il / mr.gov.il tenders (blocked / JS-only)
