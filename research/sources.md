# Research sources

Good places to discover and verify organizations. Add to this list as runs find better ones.

## Directories & databases
- Startup Nation Central Finder — https://finder.startupnationcentral.org (Israeli startups)
- Crunchbase — funding, founders, HQ
- LinkedIn company pages — size bands, locations
- Clutch / DesignRush / Sortlist — agency & studio listings (filter Israel)
- IMDb company credits — production, VFX, post on Israeli films & series

## Portfolios & awards
- Behance, Vimeo, Dribbble, ArtStation (search "Israel", "Tel Aviv")
- Awwwards / CSSDA (Israel), Motion Awards, Annecy / Animix Tel Aviv
- Effie Israel, Cannes Lions Israeli entries, Golden Pencil (Kipod)
- Ophir Awards (Israeli Academy) credits

## Press
- Geektime, CTech (Calcalist English), Globes, TheMarker, Ynet, Haaretz
- ICE (ice.co.il) and Globes marketing — ad agency account moves, campaign credits
- Portfolio magazine (prtfl.co.il) — Israeli design & studios
